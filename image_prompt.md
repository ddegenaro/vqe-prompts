# IMAGE PROMPT

Your task is IMAGE PROMPT: describe one coherent documentary still frame that could serve as a visual search example for the query. A separate retrieval text will retain its full factual identity and intent.

Select the most diagnostic visible subject, setting, action, and a few distinctive objects, clothing, architectural details, or spatial relationships. Preserve query-specified visual details and identifiable landmarks. Depict one moment at one location, never a sequence, montage, collage, or a list of alternative scenes. Write the scene directly without phrases such as 'the footage shows'.

Use a camera source or viewpoint only when specified or clearly required by the query. Otherwise use an ordinary documentary view without inventing a CCTV, drone, fisheye, or cinematic style. Keep lighting and weather consistent with explicit query cues, and leave them unspecified otherwise. Do not invent named individuals, identities, motives, outcomes, or extra dramatic objects. If an action is uncertain, depict the relevant subjects and setting without asserting the uncertain outcome.

Omit dates, statistics, analyses, requester goals, and other facts that cannot be depicted. Do not add timestamp overlays, captions, logos, or readable text, except text explicitly requested as visual evidence. Do not pad the scene with generic decorative details. Use one to three natural English sentences, usually 40–70 words and at most 90.
